ABOUT VIDYARTHI MITRA

VidyarthiMitra.org is an extensive search engine for anyone who wants precise, authentic and up-to-date information on everything related to education & career.

Universities

Information about top Universities recognized by the UGC that come under the categories: Govt, Private and Deemed.

Colleges

List and information about top colleges, recognized institutes and their courses along with their contact information.

Courses

Detailed information about Degree, Diploma & Masters and courses like Engineering, Management, Medical, Law, Architecture, Commerce, Arts, Agriculture, etc.

Entrance Exams

Up-to-date news and information about various Entrance Exams & notifications like alerts, deadlines, results & important dates.

Admissions

Alerts for application dates, eligibility criteria and admission openings to various Universities, Colleges, Institutes and their courses and streams.

Expert Advise

Career Counselling on education in India & Abroad in person and also via E-mails, Telephonic Conversations, Video Calls, etc.

Scholarship

Scholarships to help students pursue their dreams and career by supporting their education cost on the basis of academics and other factors.

Mock Exams

Students can know where they stand by taking mock tests for JEE, CET, CAT, NEET, etc by scrutinizing Performance Graphs, Self-Analysis & Expert Recommendations.

DTE

DTE & State CET Cell are Govt Agencies for Technical & Higher Education in Maharashtra. We ease admission process, by posting & explaining all recent updates.
