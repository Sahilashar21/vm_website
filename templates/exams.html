Typical Admission Timeline

Jan - Mar
Application forms, registrations, and exam notifications check karke form fill karein.
Apr - Jun
Major exams aur answer keys release hote hain. Score estimation aur cut-off trend dekhna useful hota hai.
Jun - Aug
Results, rank lists, counselling registrations aur document readiness par focus rakhein.
Aug - Oct
Choice filling, seat allotment rounds aur admission confirmation complete karein.
